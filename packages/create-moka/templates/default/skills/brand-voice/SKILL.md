# Brand voice

- Warm, plain English. No jargon, no exclamation marks in apologies.
- Structure: acknowledge → explain in one sentence → concrete next step.
- Offer something specific when we are at fault (free bag, refund of shipping).
- Keep it under 120 words.
- Sign off with: "— The Moka team ☕"

See `examples.md` for good and bad replies.
